Privacy Policy for Ratelator

Effective date: September 25, 2026

Summary: Ratelator does not collect, store, sell, or share personal information. It does not use advertising, analytics, or tracking technologies.

About this policy

This Privacy Policy explains how Ratelator (“the App”) handles information when you use it. The App is provided by [DEVELOPER OR COMPANY NAME] (“we,” “us,” or “our”).

Information we collect

We do not collect personal information through the App. The App does not require an account and does not collect your name, email address, precise location, contacts, photos, financial account information, advertising identifier, or other identifying information.

Currency amounts you enter are used only on your device to perform calculations. They are not transmitted to us or stored on our servers.

Exchange-rate service

To provide current currency conversions, the App sends the selected base currency code to the Frankfurter exchange-rate API at frankfurter.dev. Currency codes and returned exchange-rate data are not personal information.

According to Frankfurter, its public API does not collect or log personal data, IP addresses, or request URLs. Its public service uses Cloudflare for caching and protection against distributed denial-of-service attacks, with aggregate traffic statistics available to the service. Frankfurter operates independently, and its practices may change; please review its website for current information.

Information stored on your device

The App caches public exchange-rate data and related update dates locally on your device so it can display recently retrieved rates when a network connection is unavailable. This cache does not contain personal information. It is removed when you delete the App and may also be managed by the operating system.

Tracking, advertising, and analytics

The App does not contain third-party advertising, analytics SDKs, or cross-app tracking technologies. We do not sell or share personal information for advertising or profiling.

Data retention and security

Because we do not collect personal information, we do not retain personal information on our servers. Local exchange-rate cache data is protected by the security controls of your device. Internet transmissions use HTTPS; however, no method of electronic transmission or storage can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

Children’s privacy

The App is not designed to collect personal information from anyone, including children. If you believe information has been provided to us outside the App, contact us so we can address the request.

Your privacy rights

Depending on where you live, you may have rights concerning your personal information. Because the App does not collect personal information, we generally have no App-related personal information to access, correct, export, or delete. You may contact us with any privacy question or request.

Changes to this policy

We may update this Privacy Policy if the App’s features or legal requirements change. We will post the updated policy and revise the effective date above. Material changes will be communicated as required by applicable law.
